Configurations

  • Filling order: 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p 5s 4d 5p
  • s holds 2, p holds 6, d holds 10. Add the superscripts: they must equal the electron count.
  • Exceptions: Cr = [Ar] 4s¹ 3d⁵, Cu = [Ar] 4s¹ 3d¹⁰ (a half full or full d is more stable)
  • Ions: positive ions lose 4s first: Fe²⁺ = [Ar] 3d⁶. Negative ions gain into the next open spot: Cl⁻ = [Ar]
  • Rules: Aufbau builds (lowest energy first), Pauli pairs (2 per box, opposite spins ↑↓), Hund spreads (one in each box before pairing)

The four explain answers

Say or write the answer first, then tap the grey box to check. Wrong? Tap it again to mark it. Then hit Redo misses until none are left.

  • Isotopes: same number of protons, different numbers of neutrons, so different masses. Same electrons, so the same chemistry.
  • Ion electrons (Mg²⁺): protons can't change. A 2+ charge means 2 more protons than electrons, so 12 − 2 = 10 electrons.
  • 4s before 3d: 4s is lower in energy, so it fills first. But 4s is farther from the nucleus, so it loses electrons first when an ion forms.
  • Cr and Cu exceptions: one 4s electron moves into 3d because a half full (d⁵) or full (d¹⁰) d subshell is more stable.
